About Me

My name is Yassir and I am 12 years old. I am a Grade 7 student from Somalia who enjoys learning new things and exploring different ideas.

I am interested in coding, technology, and gaming. I also enjoy learning about stories, cultures, and the history behind different traditions.

I created this website to explore Somali mythical stories and share traditional tales inspired by Somali culture.

About This Website

Mythical stories are traditional tales that often include heroes, mysterious creatures, magical events, and important lessons.

Somali storytelling has a long tradition of oral stories, poetry, and legends. These stories teach people about bravery, wisdom, nature, and community.

This website contains legendary Somali stories preserved and written for this project.

Why Are Mythical Stories Important?

Mythical stories help preserve culture and traditions. They pass down important wisdom, moral lessons, and values from one generation to the next.
